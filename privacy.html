Privacy Policy

Skiney Money Studio · Effective October 11, 2026

What this app is

Skiney Money Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the Skiney Money YouTube channel. It runs on the owner's own computer and is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests access to the owner's own YouTube account in order to:

  • upload and schedule the channel's own videos (YouTube Data API);
  • read the channel's own video and channel statistics (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API).

It does not access any other user's data, and it does not read email, contacts, files, or anything outside the owner's YouTube channel.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to measure how they perform.
  • Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Skiney Money Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored tokens are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Skiney Money YouTube channel's About page: youtube.com/@SkineyMoney.
